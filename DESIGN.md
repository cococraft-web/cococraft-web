## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-caliber B2B export manufacturing identity. It pairs industrial weight and global supply-chain precision with organic agritech provenance. 

### Visual Ratios & Aesthetic Framework
- **70% Premium Industrial Manufacturing**: High-order architectural structure, razor-sharp alignment, balanced density, structural technical specs, and confident geometry.
- **20% Natural Agritech Raw Material**: Rich organic undertones derived from processed coir, coconut husk, raw compressed substrates, and lush botanical vitality.
- **10% Subtle Precision Technology**: Monospaced data markers, micrometric measurement chips, batch indicators, and technical QC certifications.

The visual style departs from standard agrarian tropes. It adopts structured editorial layouts, precise 1px architectural dividers, deep husk-toned anchor blocks, and controlled tactile surfaces that convey enterprise-scale manufacturing authority to international procurement directors.

## Colors

The palette balances deep organic browns with industrial tech greens and warm substrate neutrals.

### Roles & Application Logic
- **Primary Coconut Brown (`#4A250F`)**: Core brand anchor. Used for high-impact display typography, primary button backgrounds, master brand framing, and key industrial accents.
- **Deep Earth Container (`#2A1508`)**: High-contrast contrast zone. Reserved for industrial hero sections, full-width export metrics panels, and enterprise footer architectures.
- **Primary Natural Green (`#16833A`) & Deep Forest Green (`#075B2A`)**: Agritech indicators, export quality markers, confirmed QC badges, primary status callouts, and secondary focal actions.
- **Coconut Fibre Beige (`#C9A66B`)**: Tactical material accent. Applied to technical taglines, monospaced batch numbers, active tabs, and delicate structural borders within dark containers.
- **Warm Off White (`#F7F5EF`)**: Dominant canvas background that prevents the clinical sterility of pure white while preserving crisp contrast.
- **Primary Text (`#1C211D`) & Muted Text (`#667068`)**: Precise editorial hierarchy engineered for legibility across specification sheets and export product catalogs.
- **Soft Border (`#DDE1DB`)**: Structural grid lines, card perimeters, and technical spec divider rules.

## Typography

The typographic pairing provides architectural authority and high information density.

- **Manrope** commands headings through disciplined geometric proportion. Semi-bold and extra-bold weights give manufacturing titles an industrial, engineered weight.
- **Inter** handles high-volume specification tables, chemical analysis sheets (EC levels, expansion ratios, moisture content), and narrative copy with objective, neutral clarity.
- **Technical Code Style**: Rendered in uppercase `Inter` with extended tracking (`+0.08em`), establishing machine-grade precision for batch numbers, HS codes, container types, and lab standards.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow a modular 12-column engineering grid with structural 1px guidelines.

### Breakpoints & Columns
- **Desktop (1280px+)**: 12 columns, 32px gutters, 64px canvas margins. Max layout bounding box is 1440px.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px)**: 8 columns, 24px gutters, 40px canvas margins.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px)**: 4 columns, 16px gutters, 20px canvas margins.

### Rhythmic Layout Principles
Vertical rhythm relies on systematic multiplier steps. Section blocks use `space-2xl` to `space-3xl` gaps to enforce high-end breathing room, contrasted by compact, dense micro-spacing (`space-xs` to `space-md`) inside data-heavy industrial specifications.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained primarily through surface contrast, architectural framing, and precise micro-borders, rather than decorative drop shadows.

- **Base Level (Canvas)**: Background surface in Warm Off White (`#F7F5EF`) or Deep Earth Container (`#2A1508`).
- **Architectural Borders**: Low-contrast outlines using Soft Border (`#DDE1DB`) on light surfaces, or `rgba(201, 166, 107, 0.2)` on deep brown surfaces provide structural clarity.
- **Tactile Spec Elevation**: Raised surfaces (such as product grade cards or export order configurators) use crisp 1px borders paired with an ambient tinted shadow:
  - *Resting*: `0px 2px 8px -1px rgba(74, 37, 15, 0.05), 0px 0px 0px 1px #DDE1DB`
  - *Hover / Focused*: `0px 12px 24px -4px rgba(74, 37, 15, 0.08), 0px 0px 0px 1px #C9A66B`

## Shapes

The design uses a restrained corner radius model calibrated at `level 2` (base `8px`, intermediate `12px`, max `16px`). 

- **Inputs, Buttons, and Chips**: Strict `8px` corner radius preserves crisp manufacturing edges without aggressive brutalism.
- **Cards and Spec Containers**: Scaled to `12px` or `16px` depending on card scale, balancing modern industrial polish with approachable tactile warmth.
- **Zero Pill Shapes**: Round pill shapes are avoided to prevent a casual consumer tech appearance.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Industrial**: Background in Coconut Brown (`#4A250F`), text in Warm Off White (`#F7F5EF`), border `1px solid #4A250F`, radius `8px`. Hover shifts to `#2A1508` with subtle forward translation.
- **Agritech Secondary**: Background in Primary Natural Green (`#16833A`), text in `#FFFFFF`, radius `8px`. Active/Hover shifts to Deep Forest Green (`#075B2A`).
- **Technical Outline**: Background transparent, text in `#1C211D`, border `1px solid #DDE1DB`. Hover shifts border to `#4A250F` with `#F7F5EF` background fill.

### Technical Chips & Status Badges
- **Specification Chip**: `4px` padding vertical, `10px` horizontal. Radius `6px`. Font: Technical Code (`12px`, Semi-bold).
- **Quality / QC Verified**: Soft green background (`rgba(22, 131, 58, 0.08)`), text `#075B2A`, border `1px solid rgba(22, 131, 58, 0.2)`.
- **Grade / Batch Badge**: Beige substrate background (`rgba(201, 166, 107, 0.15)`), text `#4A250F`, border `1px solid #C9A66B`.

### Product & Material Cards
- Standard surface in `#FFFFFF`, radius `12px` to `16px`, structural border `1px solid #DDE1DB`.
- Header zone holds high-resolution fiber/substrate macro imagery; bottom tier organizes tabular specifications (pH, EC range, Compression Ratio, Moisture) framed by micro-dividers.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #DDE1DB`, radius `8px`, padding `12px 16px`.
- Placeholder text in `#667068`.
- Focus state features a sharp outline in Coconut Brown (`#4A250F`) with no chromatic glow.

### Technical Spec Tables
- Clean zebra rows alternating between `#FFFFFF` and `#F7F5EF`.
- Headers set in uppercase Technical Code with `#667068` text and bottom border `1px solid #DDE1DB`.
- Monospaced numerical values for tolerances, weights, and export volume metrics.
