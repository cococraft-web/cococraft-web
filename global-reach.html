STRATEGIC GATEWAYS

EXPORT GATEWAYS & TRANSIT EFFICIENCY

Our manufacturing plant in western Tamil Nadu benefits from equidistant multi-lane freight corridors connecting major international deepwater container terminals.

directions_boat Primary Port

V.O. Chidambaranar Port (Tuticorin)

Located approximately 260 km south of our processing facilities. An all-weather deepwater seaport with direct 24-hour feeder vessel departures to Colombo and Singapore hubs, ensuring expedited transit times to Europe, the US, and Asia.

GATE IN TRANSIT: 6–8 Hours by Container Truck
anchor Secondary Gateway

Chennai & Kattupalli Port

The premier container hub on India’s East Coast. Provides extensive direct mother-vessel calls for large-volume contractual shipments destined for the European Union, UK, East Coast North America, and Middle Eastern ports.

TRANSIT CORRIDOR: Direct NH Highway & Rail ICD Link
local_shipping Alternate Gateway

Cochin International Transshipment Terminal

Situated through the Palakkad gap adjacent to Pollachi, Cochin offers an immediate western maritime gateway for specific European direct-loop services and Mediterranean destinations.

DISTANCE: ~160 km via Palakkad Corridor

REGULATORY CLEARANCES

PHYTOSANITARY & CUSTOMS COMPLIANCE

International agricultural biosecurity requires impeccable weed-seed, fungal, and insect pest control. Every container is cleared with full government phytosanitary documentation.

verified_user

Government Phytosanitary Certificate

Issued by the Plant Quarantine Information System (PQIS), Ministry of Agriculture, Government of India, verifying freedom from prohibited nematodes and noxious weed seeds.

local_fire_department

Fumigation Certification (MB / Heat Treatment)

Conducted by licensed fumigation agencies using Methyl Bromide (MB) or certified Heat Treatment (HT 56°C for 30 minutes core) conforming to Australian Biosecurity (DAFF) & USDA APHIS standards.

pallet

ISPM-15 Hardwood Pallet Stamping

All pallets utilized for container stuffing are legally stamped with ISPM-15 marks, guaranteeing international quarantine acceptance at all destination seaports.
