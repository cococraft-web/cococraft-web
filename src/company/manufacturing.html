Every container shipped undergoes a calibrated manufacturing lifecycle to ensure stable electrical conductivity, low tannin, and certified phytosanitary cleanliness. Explore each stage below:

High-Speed Rotary Screening

Decortication & Rotary Sieving

Automated decorticating machines separate coir fibre from the spongy pith matrix. Pith is passed through calibrated 6mm and 8mm rotary trommel screens to extract ultra-fine micro-dust while keeping optimal Air-Filled Porosity (AFP).

Mesh Sieve 6mm / 8mm Trommel
Air Porosity (AFP) 18% – 25%
Micro-Dust Content < 3% Controlled
SEPARATION: Continuous Screen Calibration NEXT: Freshwater Desalination →

STAGE 03 OF 06 Biochemical Conditioning
Closed-Loop Desalination & Buffering

Freshwater Desalination & Buffering

Substrates undergo repeated freshwater immersion cycles to dissolve sodium and chloride ions. For sensitive berry and hydroponic crops, calcium nitrate buffering displaces bound potassium and sodium, stabilizing cation exchange capacity (CEC).

Target Washed EC < 0.5 mS/cm
pH Spectrum 5.8 – 6.8 Optimal
Water Standard Deep Borewell Fresh
EXTRACTION: 1:1.5 Dutch Extraction Method NEXT: Sun-Drying Aprons →

High-Tonnage Hydraulic Pressing

Automated hydraulic presses apply up to 120 tons of linear force to compress dried coir pith into compact 5kg blocks, 650g briquettes, or grow bag slabs. The 5:1 volume ratio ensures optimal freight utilization without breaking cellular walls.

LABORATORY QA

RIGOROUS IN-HOUSE TESTING PROTOCOLS

Our dedicated facility laboratory executes standardized batch assays before releasing blocks for container loading.

science

1:1.5 Dutch Extraction EC Testing

Every production batch undergoes standardized 1:1.5 water extraction testing to verify electrical conductivity levels stay consistently below 0.5 mS/cm.

EC TOLERANCE: ± 0.05 mS/cm
aspect_ratio

Volumetric Re-Expansion Assays

Randomly sampled 5kg blocks are hydrated in graduated verification vats to confirm expansion yield exceeds 75 liters of porous substrate per unit.

MINIMUM YIELD: 15 L / kg
grain

Particle Size & Sieve Analysis

Electromechanical vibratory sieve shakers verify that sub-1mm micro-particles remain under 3%, preventing compaction and root waterlogging in pots.

AIR-FILLED POROSITY: 18% – 25%
