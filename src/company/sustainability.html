CIRCULAR BIO-ECONOMY & ECOLOGICAL STEWARDSHIP

SUSTAINABLE BY DESIGN: 100% RENEWABLE COIR

Empowering the global horticulture industry to eliminate fossilized peat moss extraction. By transforming organic coconut husks into high-performance growing media, Coco Craft Exports champions biodiversity, water stewardship, and zero-waste agriculture.

forest

Peat Bog Preservation

Sphagnum peat bogs store billions of metric tons of carbon and take millennia to form. Coir replaces extracted moss with an annually renewable crop byproduct.

recycling

100% Upcycled Agricultural Husks

Rather than allowing coconut shells and husks to decompose as field waste or be incinerated, our facility converts 100% of raw inputs into marketable substrate grades.

water_drop

Closed-Loop Water Management

Washing runoff is piped into tiered sedimentation and filtration ponds, allowing sediment settling and biological water recycling for factory washing routines.

compost

Natural Biodegradability

After seasons of intensive commercial greenhouse use, spent coir substrate degrades harmlessly into rich organic compost, enriching farm soil rather than filling landfills.

ECOLOGICAL BENCHMARK

COCONUT COIR VS. SPHAGNUM PEAT MOSS

Why commercial berry growers, tomato producers, and international potting soil compounders are permanently transitioning to coconut coir substrates.

Environmental & Agronomic Metric Coco Craft Coir Substrate Traditional Sphagnum Peat Moss
Renewability & Harvest Cycle Continuous harvest every 45–60 days per tree (Zero deforestation) Takes 1,000+ years to regenerate; non-renewable fossilized wetland deposit
Carbon Footprint Impact Carbon neutral; converts organic agricultural residue Releases ancient sequestered carbon dioxide upon drainage and extraction
Re-wetting & Hydrophobicity Naturally hydrophilic; absorbs water immediately even when bone dry Highly hydrophobic when dry; requires chemical wetting agents
Structural Durability & Lignin High lignin content (45%); decomposes very slowly over 3–5 seasons Breaks down quickly into fine silt; collapses root aeration after 1 year
Natural pH Range Optimal 5.5 – 6.8 (Minimal lime adjustment required) Extremely acidic 3.5 – 4.5 (Requires heavy calcium carbonate liming)
Chemical Additives / Binding 100% natural, heat-compressed without adhesives or glues Often blended with synthetic wetting chemicals
