FOUR SUSTAINABILITY PILLARS

REGENERATIVE AGRONOMIC PRINCIPLES

Our commitment extends from local water stewardship in Pollachi to international environmental certification.

forest

Peat Bog Preservation

Sphagnum peat bogs store billions of tons of carbon and take millennia to replenish. Coir replaces extracted moss with an annually harvested coconut byproduct without habitat destruction.

100% PEAT-FREE SUBSTRATE
recycling

100% Upcycled Waste

Rather than allowing coconut husks to decompose as field waste or be incinerated, our facility upcycles 100% of raw husk volume into high-value horticultural substrates and fibre products.

ZERO BIOMASS INCINERATION
water_drop

Closed-Loop Water

Desalination wash water is routed through multi-stage concrete sedimentation settling ponds. Suspended particulates are filtered out, and over 85% of water is reused in primary washing cycles.

85% WATER RECIRCULATION
groups

Rural Empowerment

Direct integration with over 450 regional smallholder coconut farmers in Pollachi provides stable off-season revenue and safe, ethically monitored manufacturing employment.

Full Tree Utilization Blueprint

Every part of the raw coconut delivers purposeful industrial output: Coconut water and copra for nutrition, long bristle fibre for erosion control geotextiles, and spongy coir pith for premium growing media. Nothing enters landfills.
