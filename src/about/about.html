CORPORATE OVERVIEW & HERITAGE

BUILT AT THE SOURCE OF INDIA’S COIR CAPITAL

Coco Craft Exports is an international manufacturer and exporter of premium coconut coir substrates, operating with direct agricultural integration across the fertile coconut corridors of Tamil Nadu, India.

POLLACHI & ANAIMALAI AGRO-CORRIDORS

Our proximity to high-density coconut farms allows immediate processing of freshly harvested husks, guaranteeing strong cellular resiliency and structural stability in our coir pith.

OUR FOUNDATION

FROM REGIONAL EXCELLENCE TO INTERNATIONAL B2B TRUST

Founded with a clear commitment to precision engineering in sustainable growing media, Coco Craft Exports bridges the gap between natural Indian agricultural wealth and international commercial horticultural demands.

While generic agricultural brokers trade unrefined raw materials, Coco Craft Exports operates as an industrial manufacturing entity with strict quality controls: multi-cycle freshwater washing, mechanized rotary screening, continuous hydraulic compaction, and certified phytosanitary container dispatch.

Direct Origin Access

Direct tie-ups with verified Tamil Nadu plantations ensure a continuous, uninterrupted supply of mature husks year-round.

Export-Centric Compliance

Every container is packed to international specifications under Incoterms 2020 with certified fumigation and phytosanitary testing.
