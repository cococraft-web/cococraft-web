OUR FOUNDATION

FROM REGIONAL EXCELLENCE TO INTERNATIONAL B2B TRUST

Headquartered in the agricultural heartland of Tamil Nadu, Coco Craft Exports bridges the gap between South India’s rich natural coconut resources and the world’s high-tech horticultural industry. We understand that commercial growers in Germany and our international distribution partners cannot afford batch variation.

That is why every container we pack is treated as an engineered agronomic component: with verified low electrical conductivity (< 0.5 mS/cm), strict weed seed sanitation on clean concrete aprons, and calibrated Air-Filled Porosity (AFP).
