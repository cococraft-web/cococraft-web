COMPLIANCE & CERTIFICATION

EXPORT DOCUMENTATION & PHYTOSANITARY PROTOCOLS

We ensure clean customs clearance across destination borders by fulfilling all mandatory international trade standards.

verified

Phytosanitary Certificate

Issued by the Plant Quarantine Organization of India (Ministry of Agriculture). Verifies freedom from weeds, soil pests, insects, and viable seeds.

pest_control

Fumigation Certificate

Certified Methyl Bromide (MB) or Heat Treatment (HT) executed by accredited agency prior to port gate-in, as mandated by the importing country.

palette

ISPM-15 Heat Treated Pallets

Every wooden pallet carries indelible IPPC wheat stamp indicating heat treatment to 56°C core temperature for minimum 30 minutes.

receipt_long

Certificate of Origin

Legal origin documentation issued by the Chamber of Commerce / Coir Board of India for preferential customs tariffs under bilateral agreements.
