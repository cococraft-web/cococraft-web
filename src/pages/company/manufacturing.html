LIFECYCLE ARCHITECTURE

THE 6-STAGE PRECISION WORKFLOW

Every pallet dispatched by Coco Craft Exports passes through strict mechanical, biochemical, and physical controls to meet European, North American, and Australasian commercial horticultural regulations.

01 Raw Material

Selective Agro-Corridor Sourcing

Raw coconut husks are collected exclusively from mature trees across Pollachi and the surrounding Western Ghats rain-shadow belt. We inspect husk age, fibre maturity, and natural moisture to guarantee high lignin stability.

CRITERIA: Mature brown husks, 0% green rot
02 Mechanical Separation

Decortication & Rotary Sieving

Husks pass through industrial decorticators that cleanly separate long coir bristles from internal pith cells. High-capacity cylindrical rotary trommels (4mm, 6mm, and 8mm meshes) screen out unwanted fine dust and micro-fibres.

SCREENING: 4mm / 6mm standard mesh filtration
03 Key Step

Freshwater Desalination & Buffering

The pith undergoes multiple freshwater washing cycles in designated concrete percolation tanks to leach out naturally occurring potassium, sodium, and chloride ions. For pre-buffered grades, Calcium Nitrate wash displaces excess sodium.

TARGET EC: < 0.5 mS/cm (Washed) / < 1.5 mS/cm (Unwashed)
04 Sun & Mechanical

Clean Concrete Apron Sun-Drying

Washed peat is spread over 150,000+ sq.ft of elevated, sealed concrete yards under natural Tamil Nadu sunshine. By avoiding mud or dirt floors, we prevent contamination with soil pathogens, weed seeds, or external sand.

RESIDUAL MOISTURE: 15% – 18% Target Export Range
05 Compression

High-Tonnage Hydraulic Pressing

Dry raw substrate is compacted by 120-ton hydraulic compression machines into uniform 5kg blocks, 650g briquettes, or planar grow bag slabs at a 5:1 volume ratio. This minimizes ocean shipping costs while retaining rapid expansion upon re-hydration.

EXPANSION YIELD: 70 – 75 Litres per 5kg block
06 Export QA

Palletization & Phytosanitary Stamping

Blocks are stacked onto ISPM-15 certified heat-treated hardwood pallets, corner-boarded, stretch-wrapped, and strapped with high-tensile polypropylene bands. Methyl Bromide or Heat-Treatment fumigation is conducted under authorized quarantine officers.

COMPLIANCE: ISPM-15 pallets, Phytosanitary Certificate
