HOW WE MANUFACTURE

THE 6-STAGE PRECISION PROCESS

Every container shipped undergoes a calibrated manufacturing lifecycle to ensure stable electrical conductivity, low tannin, and certified phytosanitary cleanliness.

01 STAGE 1

Raw Material Sourcing

Freshly collected husks strictly selected from mature coconuts in Tamil Nadu's Pollachi and Anaimalai agricultural belts to ensure strong pith cellular structure.

check_circle Maturity QC Verified
02 STAGE 2

De-husking & Separation

Mechanized decortication cleanly segregates the long coir bristles from internal cellular coir pith, removing outer coarse fibers and oversized foreign stone debris.

check_circle Mechanized Particle Sorting
03 STAGE 3

Washing & Desalination

Controlled multiple-cycle freshwater rinsing leaches out natural potassium and sodium salts to achieve Low EC specifications (< 0.5 mS/cm) or client-defined EC grades.

check_circle Salinity & EC Target Met
04 STAGE 4

Mechanical & Sun Drying

Spread onto wide concrete-paved yards under tropical sun, followed by rotary screening drums to reduce moisture content systematically below 15%.

check_circle Moisture < 15% Verified
05 STAGE 5

Hydraulic Compression

High-tonnage hydraulic presses compress dried pith at standard 5:1 volumetric ratios into robust, damage-resistant 5kg blocks, briquettes, or custom grow slabs.

check_circle 5:1 High Compaction
06 STAGE 6

Export QC & Palletization

Final batch laboratory verification, pallet wrapping, heat-treated wooden pallet stacking, phytosanitary fumigation, and weather-sealed container dispatch.

check_circle Phytosanitary Clearance

STANDARDIZED PROTOCOLS

QUALITY BUILT INTO EVERY STAGE

Consistency is the cornerstone of industrial agritech. Every production batch is systematically tested across 4 parameters before shipping container release.

CHECK 01 grain

Raw Husk Maturity

Visual and structural fiber density checks; immediate rejection of premature or rotting husks that compromise substrate durability.

Target: 100% Mature Husks
CHECK 02 science

EC & Salinity Testing

1:1.5 extraction method calibrated lab tests conducted on every washed lot to verify electrical conductivity levels stay within client specifications.

Target: < 0.5 mS/cm (Washed)
CHECK 03 aspect_ratio

Expansion & Moisture

Random destructive sampling for expansion test yield in litres, combined with halogen moisture analyzers to certify moisture content < 15%.

Target: 14-15 Litres/KG
CHECK 04 task_alt

Export Compliance

Weed seed, pathogenic nematode, and pest absence inspections. Full phytosanitary compliance verified prior to container sealing.

Target: Zero Pathogen Tolerance
