COMMERCIAL SOLUTIONS

PROVEN SUBSTRATE PERFORMANCE FOR GLOBAL CROPS

Engineered root zone dynamics for professional growers. Whether controlling root zone temperature in European glasshouses or accelerating vegetative growth in Asian tabletop orchards, our tailored coir blends deliver predictable agronomic outcomes.

01 / COMMERCIAL GREENHOUSES

Tomatoes, Cucumbers & Bell Peppers

Commercial vine crops demand high aeration around roots, fast drainage between automated irrigation pulses, and stable physical structure that resists breakdown over a full 10-12 month harvest cycle.

Recommended Substrate Hydroponic Grow Slabs (70/30 Blend)
Target EC < 0.5 mS/cm Washed
Key Agronomic Advantage Prevent Blossom End Rot
02 / SOFT FRUIT SYSTEMS

Strawberries, Blueberries & Raspberries

Tabletop raised gutters and berry pots require rapid drainage and protection against root suffocation. Our buffered coir mixtures displace excess sodium and potassium with calcium and magnesium, creating ideal electrical equilibrium for sensitive berry root hairs.

Recommended Substrate Easy-Fill Grow Bags / 60:40 Blend
Buffering Status Super Buffered (Ca(NO3)2 Treated)
Key Agronomic Advantage Active White Root Proliferation
03 / SEEDLING PROPAGATION & NURSERIES

Wholesale Propagation & Ornamental Potted Plants

Coir pith’s high cation exchange capacity (CEC) and sponge-like moisture capillary action ensure seeds and saplings never dry out during shipping or bench holding, cutting irrigation frequency by up to 35% compared to peat moss.

Recommended Substrate 5KG Blocks / 650g Briquettes
pH Stability 5.8 – 6.5 Constant Buffer
Key Agronomic Advantage 35% Water Conservation
04 / FLORICULTURE & EPIPHYTES

Orchids, Anthuriums & Epiphytic Plants

Epiphytic and delicate flowering varieties perish in compacted or waterlogged media. Our uniformly graded coconut husk chips (Medium and Large) replicate natural tree-bark aeration, allowing roots to grip securely while draining excess moisture instantaneously.

Recommended Substrate Washed Coir Husk Chips (10-18mm)
Air-Filled Porosity 40% – 50%
Key Agronomic Advantage Zero Root Rot / Long Lifespan
